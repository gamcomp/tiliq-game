Build your next high score in the skies.

Tiliq is a strategic block puzzle set in the world of Sky Harbor. Place pieces on an 8×8 board, clear completed rows and columns, and make room for your next move. The rules are easy to learn; keeping a great run alive takes planning.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one of three available pieces onto the board
• Complete a full row or column to clear it
• Chain line clears to grow your combo multiplier
• The game ends when none of the pieces can fit

COMBOS AND POWER-UPS
Raise your combo level with smart clears and score more points. When the board gets tight, use a Hammer to clear a 3×3 area, spend two hammers on a Super Hammer that opens a row and column, or use Color Blast to remove every block of a chosen color. Rescue gives you another chance to continue.

A NEW FLIGHT EVERY DAY
New missions arrive every day: build combos, clear lines, place pieces, use the Hammer and more. Your mailbox refreshes every Monday; open a new letter each day to collect coins and power-ups. Turn on morning reminders so you never miss a day.

COMPETE AND PROGRESS
Compare your best score on world and friends rankings. Earn XP in every game, progress toward level 999, and sign in to keep your progress synced across supported devices.

MAKE SKY HARBOR YOURS
Use coins earned in the game to unlock new cargo color palettes and combo effects. Change the look of your board while keeping the same clear, responsive puzzle experience.

20 MUSIC TRACKS
Choose from 20 tracks in different styles, from lo-fi and synthwave to bossa nova and 8-bit.

Tiliq supports English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, and Dutch.

An internet connection is required.
