3. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Tiliq is a casual strategic 8x8 block-puzzle game for users aged 13+. Players place three offered pieces, clear rows/columns, plan combos, use optional power-ups, complete daily activities, progress through levels, customize visuals, and compare scores. It provides short, replayable spatial-planning and score-optimization sessions.

4. ACCESS
No credentials or sample files are required. Internet is required. Launch the app, choose English, answer consent prompts if shown, then tap “Continue as Guest.” Enter a nickname and optionally a country. Guest access includes the tutorial, Play, Daily Rewards/Missions, Store, Rankings, Friends, Settings, Account, report/hide, and deletion. Store items use coins earned through gameplay or rewarded ads; no real-money content exists. If ad inventory is unavailable, the essential game-rescue flow offers “Continue without ad”; optional coin/power-up rewards remain locked. Apple/Google sign-in is optional for sync. To report/hide, open Rankings and tap the flag beside another player. To delete, open Account > Delete Account and confirm.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Firebase Authentication (guest/Apple/Google identity), Cloud Firestore (progress, profiles, rankings, friends, moderation reports), Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, Google AdMob/UMP, Apple ATT, Apache Capacitor, and local secure storage. No payment processor, subscription platform, AI service, external data provider, or downloadable third-party gameplay content is used.

6. REGIONS

Only ad availability and consent presentation may vary by local law/inventory; declining tracking does not block gameplay.
