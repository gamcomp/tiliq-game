APP PURPOSE/AUDIENCE
Tiliq is a casual strategic 8x8 block-puzzle game for users aged 13+. It offers short spatial-planning sessions with row/column clearing, combos, optional power-ups, daily activities, progression, customization, and rankings.

USER CONTENT/DELETION
User content is limited to nickname, optional country, and score; there is no chat, messaging, posts, comments, or media upload. Nicknames are filtered. In Rankings, tap the flag beside another player to report and immediately hide them. In Account, tap Delete Account and confirm to delete the authentication identity, profile, cloud progress, friend links, and local account data.
