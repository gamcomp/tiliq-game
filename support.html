Common Questions

I signed in but don't see my old progress. Make sure you're signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used before. Progress is tied to your account, not your device.

How do I delete my account and data? Open the Account screen in Tiliq, tap Delete Account, and confirm. The in-app flow removes your authentication account, public profile, cloud progress, and associated friend links. If the App asks you to sign in again, do so and retry the deletion.

How do I report or block a player? Open Rankings and tap the flag button next to the player's name. Confirming sends the profile to our moderation queue and immediately hides that player from your rankings. You can also remove a player from your Friends list.

Does Tiliq sell coins or subscriptions? No. Tiliq currently has no real-money purchases or subscriptions. Coins are earned through gameplay and rewarded ads.
